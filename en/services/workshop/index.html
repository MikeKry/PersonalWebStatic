Where it can go next

A workshop is a good start. If you want to keep going, I am happy to help get the first agents running.

  1. 1

    Workshops

    We go through processes, pain points and ideas with your team and agree where AI brings the biggest impact. Years of leading teams help me bring sceptics on board too.

  2. 2

    Tool analysis

    I map the systems and data you use and select suitable models and technologies.

  3. 3

    Agent design

    I design agents for specific tasks: their roles, tools, rules and human approval points.

  4. 4

    Automation

    I connect the agents to your systems and put the first automated flows into production.

  5. 5

    Own platform with MCP

    A company platform with custom MCP servers through which AI works safely with your systems and data.
