AI agents & platform

From scattered data to one smooth flow.

Spreadsheets, e-mails, notes, PDFs and a legacy system. I unify all of it into one platform where AI agents process, check and pass the data on – always in the same shape and with no manual re-typing.

Inside the platform

What the agents do for you

Every agent has a clear role. Together they form a flow that runs on its own and involves people only where it really matters.

  1. 01

    Intake

    Connects to every source: mailboxes, folders, spreadsheets and the legacy system API. Nothing gets lost.

  2. 02

    Understanding

    AI recognises the document type, extracts the key data and converts it into one structure.

  3. 03

    Validation

    Data goes through your rules. Unclear cases are approved by a person with one click.

  4. 04

    Action

    The result is written to your system, a reply is sent, a task created or a report updated.

What actually changes

Today

  • Data is scattered across five different tools
  • People re-type data between systems by hand
  • Errors show up late, when they already cost money
  • Nobody has an up-to-date overview
  • Know-how lives in individual heads

With a unified flow

  • One source of truth for all data
  • Processing runs automatically, 24/7
  • Checks and approvals are part of the flow
  • Dashboards and reports update themselves
  • The process is documented, measurable and transferable
Examples

What it could look like for you

Content management

An uploaded PDF (catalogue, leaflet, document)→Ready content in the CMS, waiting for approval

The editor just uploads a PDF. AI turns it into structured pages, copy and metadata in your website format.

Website migration

An old website or outdated system→Structured content in the new CMS

Instead of weeks of copy-pasting, an agent reads, cleans, sorts and imports the content. Imports are an order of magnitude faster.

Sales

Inquiries in e-mails and Excel→Qualified lead in CRM + drafted reply

The agent reads the inquiry, enriches company info, scores it and prepares a reply ready to send.

Back office

Invoices as PDFs and scans→Booked documents with no re-typing

Invoice data is extracted, checked against orders and written into the accounting system.

Operations

Branch spreadsheets and a legacy system→One dashboard for management

Data from different sources is unified daily and management sees current numbers in one place.

Internal knowledge

Wikis, manuals and meeting notes→A company assistant that knows the answers

The team asks in plain language and gets answers with sources, connected to company data via MCP.

Principles

No revolution, just certainty

Existing systems stay where possible

Where it makes sense, nothing gets rewritten from scratch. The platform connects to what you already have and what works.

Step by step, measurably

Every process is refined in iterations: launch, measure the value, adjust. Only then do we add the next one.

Your data under control

When needed, it can be extended with permissions, logging, GDPR compliance or hosting on your own infrastructure.
