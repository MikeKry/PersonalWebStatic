Focus areas

What I can do for you

AI delivers the most value when it is connected to your data and processes. That is what I specialise in.

Primary focus

AI integration into existing systems

I connect language models to your CRM, ERP, CMS, databases and internal tools – without rewriting what already works.

  • LLM integration (OpenAI, Claude, Azure OpenAI) via API
  • Smart search and chat over company data (RAG)
  • Data extraction from documents, e-mails and invoices
  • Permissions, security and data protection

AI agents & automation

Agents that handle repetitive tasks for you – from triaging requests to preparing documents and reports.

  • Design and development of tool-using AI agents (MCP, function calling)
  • Workflow automation across systems
  • Human-in-the-loop approval where it matters
  • Monitoring, logging and measuring savings

New AI-powered systems

I design and build applications where AI is part of the core – not just a bolted-on chatbot.

  • System architecture and design
  • Backend, APIs and databases
  • Internal tools and portals
  • Fast prototype → stable production

AI company transformation

I guide the company through the whole journey: from workshops and agent design to your own AI platform.

  • Team workshops and process analysis
  • Tool analysis, model and technology selection
  • Custom platform tools and MCP servers
  • Roadmap, training and team handover

Process
